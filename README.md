# Clear Vision Hub

Based on the reference template (Dermato Webflow template) from your video, here is a breakdown of its core UI/UX structure adapted into our luxury dark aesthetic (#000926, #0F52BA, #A6C5D7, #D6E6F3) for Haripriyaa Netralaya.




The template excels at editorial serif typography, floating pill badges, Bento grids, and 3-image service accordions. Here is the page-by-page design plan.




Page 1: Home Page (Main Conversion Hub)

Sticky Floating Header (Framed Pill / Navbar):




Center: Clean branding text HARIPRIYAA NETRALAYA with a subtle sapphire eye icon.

Links: Home, About, Treatments, Technology, Reviews, Contact.

Right Action: Book Appointment (Pill button with #0F52BA solid glow).

Hero Section (Editorial Split Banner):




Top Tag: Pill badge [ ★ 4.9 Rated Ophthalmology Center in Islampur ] (#A6C5D7 border on #051336).

Central Headline (Large Serif): "Clear, healthy vision starts here." (Crisp #D6E6F3 / White).

Subtext: "Advanced stitchless cataract surgery and comprehensive eye diagnostics tailored for precision."

Floating Social Proof Cards (Left & Right):




Left Pill: "MS / Board Certified Surgeon"

Right Glass Card: Review quote from a patient + 5 Gold Stars.

Main Visual: High-resolution close-up of clear vision / eye examination framed in a smooth rounded curve container.

Section 2: Mission Statement & Mini Bento (Trust Intro):




Pill Tag: EXPERT EYE CARE FOR EVERY AGE

Large Headline: "We combine advanced surgical ophthalmology with compassionate care to restore sharp, comfortable vision."

Floating Mini-Cards: 3 overlapping curved image cards showing consultation, slit-lamp exam, and the optical store, flanked by a 4.9 ★ (34+ Google Reviews) trust badge.

Section 3: Signature Treatments (3-Image Horizontal Rows):




Modeled directly after the template's service blocks (Title on top, brief description, View Detail pill button, and a 3-image thumbnail strip below):




Painless Cataract Surgery (Phaco/MICS): Stitchless, injection-free, rapid recovery.

Retina & Diabetic Eye Care: Laser diagnostics and pressure management.

Glaucoma & Vision Screening: Automated visual field testing.

In-House High-Definition Opticals: Anti-glare, blue-filter lenses, and designer frames.

Section 4: The Bento Grid ("Care That Puts You First"):




A 6-box high-tech bento grid layout in dark glass cards (#030d2e):




Box 1 (Tall Left): Before/After Vision Clarity Simulation (Blurry vs. Crystal Clear).

Box 2: Seamless Hospital Experience (Zero waiting OPD workflows).

Box 3 (Metric): 9+ Years of Clinical Dedication | 2,000+ Screenings.

Box 4 (Metric): 99% Patient Satisfaction Rate with floating quote cards.

Box 5: Personalized Lens Selection (Monofocal / Multifocal).

Box 6 (Wide Bottom): Advanced Phaco Machine Spotlight.

Section 5: Doctor Profile Spotlight:




Clean 2-column layout:




Left: Large framed doctor portrait with name & credentials (MBBS, MS Ophthalmology).

Right: Key principles (Painless Methods, Certified Surgical Tech, Transparent Guidance) + Our Approach CTA.

Section 6: Live Google Reviews Carousel:




Header: "What our patients say" with left/right arrow controls.

Big Right Card: Rating summary (4.9 ★ Clients Rating with verified badge).

Horizontal Testimonial Cards: Patient name, locality (Islampur / Sangli), and verified review quote.

Section 7: Instant Booking Strip & FAQ Accordion:




Left: Interactive FAQ drop-downs (e.g., "Is cataract surgery painful?", "How long does recovery take?", "Do you accept health insurance?").

Right Action Card: "Still have questions? Chat on WhatsApp" button.

Footer (Pre-Footer CTA + Navigation):




Curved banner: "Transform your vision with us" + Book an Appointment button.

Bottom columns: Clinic address (Near Shivparvati Theatre, Peth-Sangli Road), Phone, WhatsApp, OPD Hours, and Quick Links.

Page 2: About & Doctors Page

Hero Banner: "Excellence in Eye Care for Uran Islampur" with background clinic ambient photography.

Doctor Profile Details: In-depth medical background, surgical certifications, and philosophy.

Clinic Infrastructure Tour: 4-grid photo gallery of the Operation Theatre, OPD Consultation Room, Diagnostic Lab, and Optical Display Counter.

Page 3: Treatments / Services Detail Page

Hero Grid: Overview of all specialty departments.

Dedicated Treatment Cards: Each service featuring a symptom checklist, step-by-step procedure explanation, recovery timeline, and technology used.

Direct Service Booking: Dedicated Consult for this Treatment button linked directly to WhatsApp with pre-filled text.

Page 4: Technology & Equipment Showcase

Visual Machine Showcase: Clean spotlight cards featuring equipment name, photo, and real patient benefit (e.g., Automated Non-Contact Tonometer for pressure check with zero eye contact).

Sterilization & Safety Standards: Highlighting hospital hygiene and sterile OT protocols.

Page 5: Contact, Map & Appointment Booking

Interactive Split Layout:




Left Form: Dark glass booking form (Patient Name, Phone, Service, Preferred Date & Time, Doctor Notes).

Right Info Box:




Live embedded Google Maps card with direct navigation link.

OPD Schedule table (Morning & Evening sessions).

Direct One-Tap Call and WhatsApp consultation buttons.
